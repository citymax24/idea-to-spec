## Notes

- This is content, not visual, so it goes through the spec and not through the canvas — the two-lane rule in `design/README.md`. SCR-08 and the clickable prototype are rebuilt after `/speckit-idea-apply`, not before.
- R3-01 replaces an assumption with a decision. A-06 ("only the Chef administers users") was never backed by a source; it came from R1-01, where the reviewer asked for the screen but named no role. Seeing the last-Chef refusal in the prototype is what exposed it.
- **Open in the wording**: "notwendig" can mean two different things, and they lead to different specs.
  - *allow*: a company **can** have several administrators; one is enough to start. Nothing forces a second.
  - *require*: a company **must** have at least two, so nobody can be locked out — that would need a rule at setup and a prompt when the second-to-last admin is removed.
  The reviewer chose *require* at confirmation. The item was rewritten before it was applied: a minimum of two administrators, a setup step that names the second, and a refusal that bites at the second-to-last, not the last.
