## Scope of this extraction

This corpus is 764 KB of legislation and specification. Extracting every atomic claim from it would produce thousands of rows, most about matters this loop cannot touch — conformity assessment procedures, notified bodies, CE marking, market surveillance, penalties, delegated acts. Those are real obligations on an economic operator; they are not decidable while writing a spec or clicking a prototype.

What was mined, and what was not:
