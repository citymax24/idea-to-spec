# Open Questions

Feature: `standards/accessibility-eaa` · 2026-09-05

Gaps no source fills. Each question names the spec section that carries an assumption until it is answered.

| ID | Question | Why it matters | Section affected | Suggested default | Resolution |
|----|----------|----------------|------------------|-------------------|------------|
| Q-01 | Is an idea built in this repository in scope of the EAA at all? Article 2(2) is a closed list of consumer services — electronic communications, audiovisual access, named transport elements, consumer banking, e-books, e-commerce — and a consumer is a natural person acting outside their trade or profession. | Decisive. A tool sold to businesses is not a consumer service and is not on the list; the legal duty would then not attach at all, and everything this module enforces would be a voluntary choice rather than a requirement. The first dry-run idea in this repository, a quote tracker for landscaping firms, looks squarely outside it. | §2 Applicability | Bind the module to every idea anyway, and treat legal scope as a separate question answered per idea in its exemption record. Accessibility above the legal minimum is a defensible decision; silently assuming the law applies is not. | open |
| Q-02 | Does the microenterprise relief apply to the operator of a given idea? | Article 4(5) and BFSG § 3(3) exempt microenterprises providing services — under 10 people and at most EUR 2 million turnover or balance sheet — from the service accessibility requirements entirely. | §2 Applicability | Do not assume it. It is a fact about the operator, not about the product, and it can stop being true between one release and the next. | open |
| Q-03 | Which version of EN 301 549 applies, and which WCAG level does it reference? | It is the missing link in the citation chain. The law says "perceivable, operable, understandable, robust"; EN 301 549 is what turns that into a testable level. Without it the module asserts the bridge from WCAG 2.1 AA to the legal duty rather than citing it. | §1 Purpose and legal basis, §3 Requirements | V3.2.1 referencing WCAG 2.1 level AA, which is what the module is currently written against. Fetch the document (see `inputs/SOURCES.md`) and confirm. | open |
| Q-04 | Has a newer EN 301 549 referencing WCAG 2.2 been published and cited in the Official Journal? | A bump would add success criteria — target size, dragging movements, consistent help among them — and would move every idea bound to this module. This is the drift the registry exists to surface. | §3 Requirements | Assume V3.2.1 / WCAG 2.1 AA until confirmed otherwise, and keep the three WCAG 2.2 criteria as `recommended` rather than `required`. | open |
| Q-05 | Does this module bind level AA only, or selected AAA criteria as well? | EAA5 carries 78 criteria; 50 are A or AA and are extracted here. AAA is never required as a whole by any accessibility standard, but individual AAA criteria are sometimes adopted deliberately. | §1, §3 | A and AA only. Anything above goes in as `recommended`, numbered from 101. | open |
| Q-06 | For each of the 50 criteria, is it decidable while writing a spec, while clicking a prototype, or only in built software? | This is the `Bites` field of every requirement, and it decides what a green checklist actually proves. A criterion about live captions or about assistive-technology compatibility cannot be settled by a single-file HTML prototype. | §3 Requirements | Classify each one when drafting the module spec, and mark `build` generously rather than optimistically. | open |
| Q-07 | What did the BFSGV amendment of 10 July 2026 change? | It is two months old at the time of this intake and is included in the stored text, but nobody has compared it against the previous version. | §1, §3 | Treat the stored text as current and check the amendment before the module is accepted. | open |
| Q-08 | Does this organisation intend to ever rely on the disproportionate-burden relief? | If yes, Article 14 requires a documented assessment, kept five years, renewed whenever the service changes and at least every five years — and outside funding for accessibility forfeits the relief entirely. That is an ongoing obligation, not a one-off argument. | §2 Applicability | No. Relying on it costs more process than meeting the criteria, at the scale of the ideas this repository produces. | open |
| Q-09 | In what form must the accessibility information required by the directive be published? | Annex I § III(b) and § III(d) require information about the accessibility of the service in accessible modes. The exact form for a private-sector service is the part of this module least settled by the sources read. | §3 Requirements | A reachable page stating which requirements are met, the known gaps, and how to report a barrier. | open |
